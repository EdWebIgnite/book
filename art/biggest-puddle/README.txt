Corrected complete artwork package, 13 November 2026

CONTENTS
page1.png through page12.png, cover.png, characters.png.
All 12 story pages, the cover and the approved character sheet are included.
The approved page2.png has been restored.

EXPORT DIMENSIONS
page1.png through page11.png: 3200 x 2000 pixels.
page12.png: 6400 x 2000 pixels, 3.2:1 panorama.
cover.png: 1600 x 2400 pixels.
characters.png: approved original at 1536 x 1024 pixels.

EXPORT METHOD
Images were proportionally enlarged using high-quality bicubic resampling.
This corrects file dimensions; it does not create new painted detail.
No image was stretched or squeezed.
The panorama received a small centred top-and-bottom crop, approximately
23 source pixels per edge, to change 3:1 to 3.2:1. All three ducklings and
all finding targets remain visible.
Original-resolution PNGs are preserved separately in the local project's
artwork_originals folder.
dimensions.json records the original sizes and the export crops.
